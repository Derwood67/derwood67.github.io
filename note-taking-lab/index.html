      Deep reading notes

      Deep Reading Notes

      Notes for: —

      Write new or tricky words. Use clues in the text to figure out the meaning.

      WordWhat it meansClue or sentence from the text

      Follow the plot mountain. Short phrases in order.

      The main idea is what the whole text or section is mostly about. Details prove it.

      Theme is the lesson or message the author wants you to learn. It fits real life, not just this story.

      Point of view is who tells the story. The author's point of view is what the author thinks or wants you to feel.

      An inference is a smart guess. The author doesn't say it, so you use clues from the text + what you already know.

      Example
      It says…Lena hated being told to wait and stepped onto the slippery bridge.
      +
      I know…People who rush sometimes take risks without thinking.
      =
      So I infer…Lena is impatient, and that almost got her in trouble.

      Smart notes do this

      • Write key words, not whole sentences.
      • Put ideas in your own words.
      • Keep names, dates, numbers and bold words.
      • Add a question mark ? next to something confusing.
      • Leave space to add more later.

      Skip these

      • Copying the whole paragraph.
      • Fun extras that don't change the big idea.
      • Repeated information you already wrote.
      • Little words like "the," "very," "really."
