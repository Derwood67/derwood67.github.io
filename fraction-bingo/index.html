      How to play

      1. Write your card number on your whiteboard or paper.
      2. Solve each problem the teacher calls. Show your work.
      3. If your answer is on your card, tap it to mark it.
      4. Five in a row across, down, or corner to corner? Call BINGO!

      Answers are written the way you'd find them after solving. Some answers are not simplified, so look for the exact form.
