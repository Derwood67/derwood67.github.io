Control Center

Every interactive app in one place. Open starts the app in a new tab, just as students see it. Copy link gives you the link to post in Google Classroom. Update opens Claude with a request for that app already filled in; type the change and send. Tap the ☆ to pin the apps you use most to the top.

Student links never need to be re-sent: when an app is updated, the same link opens the newest version. Apps marked claude.ai open on claude.ai. Private apps hold student records, so they have no public link. Teacher dashboards are inside each app; open the app and choose Teacher dashboard.
