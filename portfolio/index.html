Learning apps built for real students

I'm Darren Echols, a 4th and 5th grade teacher. I design and build interactive learning tools for my own classroom. Each one starts from a standard and a problem I saw with real students: who needs more practice, who is ready to move on, and what the teacher needs to see.

Approach

What the work shows

Patterns that run across the apps below.

Standards alignment

Every app is tagged to grade-level standards, with a switch for Common Core and other states' standards.

Mastery-based progress

Students unlock the next section by showing mastery, with a short lesson after a miss.

Adaptive practice

Questions get easier after a miss and harder after a correct answer.

Data for teachers

Dashboards, score-by-standard charts, words-correct-per-minute tracking and printable reports.

Access for every learner

Speech recognition for decoding, read-aloud voices, a bilingual studio and offline use.

Interactive apps

Apps students use on their own or play together as a class. Each card opens a working version: choose Try it as a guest to explore without a class name, or Watch Demo for a narrated tour.

Teacher tools

Planning, grading and content-building tools I use behind the scenes.

Curriculum materials

Worksheets, answer keys and printable review packets I wrote to go with my units.
