About

I teach a combined 4th and 5th grade class in Mr. Echols' Room 2. When the resources I had didn't fit my students, I started building my own: reading apps for the novels we study, fraction games that lock the next level until a student shows mastery, writing tools that give instant feedback on tense and punctuation, and dashboards that show me exactly where each student stands.

The work sits where teaching, instructional design and product thinking meet. I plan from the standard, design for the student who is struggling and the one who is ahead, test with real kids, and keep improving from what the data shows.
